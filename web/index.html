On embedded targets (ESP32, Teensy 4.1 i.MXRT1062, and Raspberry Pi), keypads are omitted. A single physical toggle/slide switch drives the state machine: Switch ON slides up the escalation ladder (increasing Randomness Delta ΔR and Haas spatial dilation); Switch OFF triggers an automatic 5-minute (300-second) smooth de-escalation down to silence.

Target Pinouts & Source Locations

1. Teensy 4.1 (NXP i.MXRT1062 @ 600 MHz ARM Cortex-M7)
   - Source: firmware/teensy41_imxrt1062/teensy41_imxrt1062.ino
   - Switch Pin: Digital Pin 2 (INPUT_PULLUP -> GND)
   - Optional Analog Slide Pot: Pin A0 (Pin 14)
   - Audio Out: I2S Teensy Audio Shield OR MQS (Pins 10 & 12 Stereo)

2. ESP32 (Dual-Core Xtensa LX6 / RISC-V)
   - Source: firmware/esp32_escalator/esp32_escalator.ino
   - Switch Pin: GPIO 4 (Internal Pull-Up -> GND)
   - Audio Out: Built-in Stereo 8-bit DAC (GPIO 25 Left, GPIO 26 Right)
     or external PCM5102A I2S DAC (BCLK=27, LRCK=14, DOUT=22)

3. Raspberry Pi (Zero 2W / 3 / 4 / 5)
   - Source: firmware/rpi_gpio_daemon.py (or native Rust binary `--switch-mode`)
   - Switch Pin: BCM GPIO 17 (Physical Pin 11 -> GND Pin 9)
   - Audio Out: 3.5mm Headphone Jack / USB Audio / I2S HAT
